# Veda Luxe: client brief (as supplied by the user)

Positioning: Premium Ayurveda-rooted skincare; luxury clean beauty; rituals + ingredient provenance storytelling.

## 1. Target persona: "The Conscious Ritualist" (primary)
- Age 25-38, urban metros (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad)
- Income: INR 12L+ household; willing to pay premium for efficacy + provenance
- Mindset: reads INCI lists; trusts Ayurvedic heritage but demands clinical proof
- Platforms: Instagram (daily), YouTube (reviews), Reddit/Backend for research
- Triggers: clean-label claims, no nasties, dermat validation, aesthetic packaging
- Pain points: greenwashing; "Ayurvedic" brands with filler formulas; slow results
- Buying behaviour: researches 2-3 creators before purchase; repeat LTV customer

## 3. Competitor brands (and the angle)
- Kama Ayurveda: heritage Ayurveda, premium pricing, ITC-backed. Edge: tried-and-tested minimalist curation; trusted aestheticians.
- Forest Essentials: luxury Ayurveda, temple/heritage aesthetic, gifting. Edge: sensory luxury experience and physical retail presence.
- Just Herbs: clean Ayurveda, D2C-led, strong IG community. Edge: affordable entry to clean Ayurveda, founder-led trust.
- Ohria Ayurveda: boutique Ayurveda, small-batch, royal patronage story. Edge: storytelling depth, artisanal scarcity narrative.
- Sadhev: luxury Ayurveda (Lakme founders' family), gift-box focus. Edge: celebrity/founder visibility, gifting bundles.

## 4. Non-negotiables, brand side
1. No hidden parabens, sulphates, mineral oil or synthetic fragrance in hero SKUs
2. Every claim substantiated (clinical/consumer study); no unverified % claims
3. Never pay for a positive-review guarantee; editorial freedom stays with the creator
4. No AI-generated imagery of faces in campaign content
5. All content tagged #ad / ASCI-compliant disclosures

## 5. Non-negotiables, influencer side
1. Genuine prior use or 4-week trial before review content
2. No simultaneous promotion of a direct clean-Ayurveda competitor within 30 days
3. Engagement authenticity: under 20% suspicious follower rate, real comment quality
4. Never name a competitor in comparison content under our sponsorship
5. Share reach/screenshot analytics within 7 days of posting

## 6. Outreach playbook (for LATER; not used in a search-only run)
- DM/Email subject: "Veda Luxe x [Name] - ritual-first collaboration"
- First-line hook: reference one specific post of theirs (ingredient take, routine video)
- Offer structure: product seeding first; then paid reel + story bundle; affiliate code 10-15%
- Key selling point: category-first provenance lab; creator gets a co-branded ritual kit
- Avoid: mass-blast templates; asking for unpaid UGC; 24h deadlines
- Follow-up cadence: day 2 gentle bump; day 5 final note; then move to waitlist
